# Privacy policy: Brahmi Keyboard

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Brahmi Keyboard is an input method (keyboard) for Android.

**What the keyboard handles.** Everything you type passes through the keyboard so that it can be converted and sent to the app you are using. This happens entirely on your phone.

**What it does not do.** The keyboard has no internet permission. It cannot send anything off your device. It collects no personal data, has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not read the content of your fields except the word you are currently typing and, for the Delete key, the text just before the cursor.

**Password fields.** In password fields the preview shows asterisks. The text you type is passed to the app unchanged.

**What is stored.** Only your settings (mode, languages, size, position and colours), kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

**Children.** The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this repository with a new date.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/init-india/brahmikeyboard/issues
