Brahmi Keyboard lets you type Ashokan Brahmi, the script of the edicts of Ashoka, on your phone, using the Indian language you already know.

THREE MODES
• Custom (cus): turns what you type into another script. Type with English letters or with a language's own keys, and choose the output script. English to English leaves your text unchanged.
• Brahmi (br): the keys show your language's letters with the Brahmi letter in small type. Type your language and the app receives Ashokan Brahmi.
• Pure Brahmi (pb): the keys are Brahmi letters, with your language's reading in small type.

Hold the Mode key for quick English, and hold it again to come back.

MADE FOR LEARNERS OF THE SCRIPT
• 21 Indian languages: Assamese, Bengali, Bodo, Dogri, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Kashmiri, Konkani, Maithili, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi, Santali, Sindhi, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu.
• A preview strip shows your word in your language and in Brahmi before it is sent.
• Conjuncts are stacked with a joiner key, written the Ashokan way.
• Ashokan numerals, which are additive and have no zero.
• Letters that do not belong to Ashokan script are refused with a red message. Post-Ashokan letters such as श and ष, and ऐ and औ, give an orange warning.
• A built-in user guide explains the script and every key.

COMFORT
• Change key height, width, position and the space under the keys, so the bottom row never sits on your phone's back and home buttons.
• Choose keyboard, key and letter colours, with light and dark themes.
• A Go key runs the app's own action, and Enter starts a new line.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No network access, no ads, no analytics, no accounts. Everything is converted on your phone. In password fields the preview shows asterisks.
